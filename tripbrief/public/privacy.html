Privacy Policy

Trip Brief · Last updated 29 August 2026

Summary: Trip Brief has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no backend server. It does not collect, store or transmit personal information about you to us, because there is no “us” server to send it to.

What data the app handles

Trip Brief runs entirely in your browser (or, on Android, inside a Trusted Web Activity that renders the same web app). It has no server component of its own.

  • Search terms. City names you type are sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding service to resolve coordinates.
  • Coordinates. The coordinates of destinations you select are sent to the weather, air quality and encyclopedia services listed below.
  • Your approximate location. On first load the app calls GeoJS, which returns an approximate country based on your IP address. This is used only to pick a sensible default currency and to flag driving-side differences. It is never stored or transmitted anywhere else.

What is stored on your device

  • localStorage — your theme, temperature unit and preferred currency.
  • sessionStorage — a short-lived cache of API responses so the app does not repeatedly call free public services. Cleared when you close the tab.
  • A service worker cache holding the app's own files and recently viewed destination data, so it works offline.

All of this stays on your device. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Third-party services

Fetching data necessarily discloses your IP address and the request itself to these providers, each of which has its own privacy policy:

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — place search and map tiles
  • Open-Meteo — weather, historical climate, air quality
  • REST Countries — country profiles
  • Frankfurter — exchange rates
  • Nager.Date — public holidays
  • Wikimedia / Wikipedia — summaries and landmarks
  • Open Disease — public health statistics
  • GeoJS — approximate country from IP

Children

Trip Brief is a general-audience travel reference tool. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
